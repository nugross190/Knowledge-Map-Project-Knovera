# TKA template: Sejarah

Describes the TKA 2025 Sejarah paper (pilihan, 30 items, numbers 1–30, none missing) as 8 item types. Codes, levels and examples are in `data/tka-types.json`.

## 1. The paper at a glance

- **One short stimulus per item.** Each item opens with a short passage on one event, figure or policy (passage and stem together 32–82 words). Three items add a figure (SEJ25-003 a painting of a Dutch landing, 010 a Borobudur relief photograph, 017 an illustration of poenale sanctie) and three a table (005 Tritura, 025 economic programmes of Demokrasi Liberal, 030 dualisme kepemimpinan). Six print a source line (005, 010, 017, 025, 027, 029). There are no shared stimuli.
- **Formats.** 14 `pilihan-ganda`, 9 `pilihan-ganda-kompleks` ("Jawaban benar lebih dari satu"), 7 `benar-salah`. The two-column headings vary: Benar / Salah (005, 021, 024), Tepat / Tidak Tepat (014, 016, 028), Sesuai / Tidak Sesuai (025).
- **Duplicates.** Two items are printed twice: the Demak item (number 7) also on page 2 under item 2, and the Aceh item (number 9) also on page 4 under the indicator box of item 4. Each is recorded once.
- **Content.** Each item names an *elemen*, from Pengantar Ilmu Sejarah through Orde Baru sampai Reformasi. Several indicator boxes are cut off or do not match their item (see section 4). The item type follows what the item asks.
- **Much rests on recall.** In many items the stimulus sets the scene but does not contain the deciding fact (SEJ25-009, 011, 015, 019, 022): the student must know it.

## 2. Rules for every Sejarah item

- **Every date, name and number is checked against a cited textbook or source,** and `alasan` names it. The paper has doubtful facts: SEJ25-027 prints "10 Desember 1948" for the start of Agresi Militer Belanda II, which the transcriber notes is usually given as 19 Desember 1948; 029 credits a quote on Politik Mercusuar to Kahin's *Nationalism and Revolution in Indonesia*, a book on the revolution up to 1949.
- **An option is wrong as a whole or right as a whole.** SEJ25-002 option B ("menghasilkan lada dan pala") turns on one word: Banten produced pepper, but nutmeg came from Maluku/Banda. A student who knows the main fact is marked wrong for a detail. Put the error in the main claim.
- **The stem's wording must fit every keyed option.** SEJ25-023 asks for struggles "menghadapi Belanda", but one keyed option, Pertempuran 10 November 1945, was fought mainly against Sekutu (Inggris). If only direct battles with Belanda count, the item has one key while its stem says "Jawaban benar lebih dari satu".
- **A table or figure must be needed, and must hold every fact the options use.** SEJ25-005's Tritura table does not help decide which events began Orde Baru; 030's table on dualisme kepemimpinan does not bear on party simplification. In 025 statement 1 adds a cause ("akibat munculnya berbagai macam konflik") that the table does not give.
- **Benar-salah statements must each be decidable.** SEJ25-024 has all three statements keyed Benar, and statement 3 ("Lebih mementingkan peperangan dalam mengeksploitasi sumber daya alam") is too vague to judge.
- **Spell names one way and correctly.** SEJ25-009 alternates "Abdul Jalil" and "Abdul Djalil"; 011 prints "Samura Pasai".
- **Indonesian textbook terms.** Use *sinkronik*, *diakronik*, *sumber primer*, *sumber sekunder*, *latar belakang*, *dampak*, as the Kurikulum Merdeka books do.

## 3. Item types

### SEJ-FAKTA: Fakta, peristiwa, atau bukti dalam suatu kategori (level 1)

- **Asks:** which events, evidence, routes or cultural traces belong to a stated category (start of Orde Baru, proof of the Gujarat theory, Spanish route, Islamic traces in Jawa, physical struggles after 1945, features of Japanese rule).
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda`, `pilihan-ganda-kompleks`, `benar-salah`.
- **Stem patterns:** "Manakah yang termasuk dalam peristiwa penting terkait titik awal lahirnya Orde Baru?" · "Apa bukti yang mendukung teori Gujarat tersebut?" · "Manakah jalur pelayaran Bangsa Spanyol sehingga sampai di Nusantara?" · "Manakah dari pernyataan berikut yang menunjukkan fakta yang tepat dan tidak tepat tentang jejak budaya Islam di Jawa?"
- **Key:** a textbook fact named in `alasan` with its source: nisan Sultan Malik as-Saleh at Samudra Pasai for the Gujarat theory (011); the Atlantic–Amerika–Pasifik–Filipina–Maluku route (015).
- **Distractors:** the same kind of fact from another period, region or religion (016, statement 3, Tari Sanghyang, a Hindu-Bali tradition; 023, Pertempuran 5 Hari di Semarang against Jepang and the Andi Azis and RMS rebellions); the rival's version (015, options D and E, the Portugis route by Tanjung Harapan); an event that never happened as stated (005, statement 3, "Penetapan Dewan Jenderal oleh Presiden Soekarno").
- **Watch:** level 1 here means recall of a single fact, not reading it from the stimulus. Keep each option to one checkable fact.
- **Examples:** SEJ25-005, 011, 015, 016, 023, 024.

### SEJ-KONSEP: Konsep dasar ilmu sejarah (level 2)

- **Asks:** apply a concept of the discipline to a passage: berpikir sinkronik or diakronik, or what an expert's definition of history stresses.
- **Stem patterns:** "Berdasarkan informasi di atas, manakah pernyataan yang sesuai dengan cara berpikir sejarah sinkronik?" · "Apakah fokus pengertian sejarah menurut pendapat tersebut?"
- **Key:** the concept's defining feature: sinkronik looks at many aspects within one period (SEJ25-001, option E); Herodotus describes history as moving in a cycle and repeating (006, option E).
- **Distractors:** the paired concept (diakronik options B, C, D in 001); other definitions of history (subjective interpretation, a systematic collection of facts, literature, oral tradition in 006).
- **Examples:** SEJ25-001, 006.

### SEJ-SEBAB: Latar belakang, faktor, dan tujuan (level 2)

- **Asks:** why an event happened: the trigger of a resistance, the factors behind a port's rise, the aim of a voyage.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda` or `pilihan-ganda-kompleks`.
- **Stem patterns:** "Apa saja faktor yang mempengaruhi perkembangan ekonomi Kerajaan Banten ...?" · "Apa tujuan utama Bangsa Belanda datang ke Nusantara ?" · "Apakah yang menjadi alasan perlawanan Teuku Abdul Jalil terhadap Jepang di Aceh?" · "Peristiwa apa yang terjadi di Laut Tengah sehingga melatarbelakangi kedatangan bangsa Eropa ke Nusantara?"
- **Key:** the specific cause the textbooks give: seikerei for the Aceh resistance (009); the fall of Konstantinopel to Turki Usmani in 1453 (019); the search for spice-producing regions (003).
- **Distractors:** a real policy of the same occupier that was not the trigger (009: guseikanbu, Jugun Ianfu, Kumiai, Tencosetsu); a policy of another era (022, options D and E, sewa tanah and cultuur stelsel); a kingdom from the wrong context (002, option D, Majapahit; 007, option B, Banten); a general motive in place of the event asked for (019, options A–D).
- **Watch:** list causes that are either clearly true or clearly false. In SEJ25-007 option C (a list of Demak's export commodities) is unchecked and may be meant as a key.
- **Examples:** SEJ25-002, 003, 007, 009, 019, 022.

### SEJ-DAMPAK: Dampak atau akibat (level 2)

- **Asks:** the consequence of a war, conquest, colonial practice or political document.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda`, `pilihan-ganda-kompleks`, `benar-salah`.
- **Stem patterns:** "Bagaimanakah pengaruh konflik antara Mataram dengan Surabaya terhadap kehidupan ekonomi di Nusantara?" · "Bagaimana dampak perang tersebut dalam kehidupan ekonomi di Nusantara?" · "... manakah di antara pernyataan berikut yang paling tepat menunjukkan pengaruh praktik poenale sanctie terhadap kehidupan pekerja pada masa kolonial?" · "Bagaimana akibat Supersemar sehingga mendorong lahirnya Orde Baru?"
- **Key:** the effect the outcome implies for the winner and for the loser (018: Gowa-Tallo gains control, Bone weakens).
- **Distractors:** the reversed effect (018, options B and D); a role that contradicts the polity's nature (013, options A and E, Mataram as a maritime trade centre, while the item's `alasan` calls it agrarian); an overstated effect (028, statement 2, Supersemar as a mandate to be Presiden, while `alasan` notes Soeharto became Pejabat Presiden in 1967 and Presiden in 1968).
- **Watch:** with a figure, the key must follow from what is shown. In SEJ25-017 option A ("kehilangan kemerdekaannya") is as defensible as the key E.
- **Examples:** SEJ25-013, 017, 018, 028.

### SEJ-STRATEGI: Strategi, respons, atau langkah (level 2)

- **Asks:** how a people or government responded to a situation: a strategy of resistance, a response to an attack, a policy step.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda`, `pilihan-ganda-kompleks`, `benar-salah`.
- **Stem patterns:** "Strategi apa yang digunakan rakyat Maluku untuk melawan Portugis?" · "Apa bentuk respons strategis bangsa Indonesia dalam mempertahankan kemerdekaan setelah Agresi Militer Belanda II?" · "Apa saja langkah-langkah pemerintah dalam mengatasi masalah partai politik?"
- **Key:** the responses actually taken: PDRI, gerilya and diplomasi (027); fusion of four Islamic parties into PPP and five non-Islamic parties into PDI (030).
- **Distractors:** the opponent's tool presented as resistance (021, statement 3, a monopoly treaty with Portugis); an alliance with the enemy's creations (027, option D, negara-negara boneka); a step that predates the problem (030, option A, Sekber Golkar, formed in 1964 according to `alasan`).
- **Watch:** SEJ25-027's stimulus lists all three responses, so the item is a reading task; make the student infer the response from events, not copy it. 021 statement 1 (cooperation with rulers of Papua, Jawa and Sumatra) is keyed Salah but the transcriber notes Ternate's alliances may make it Benar.
- **Examples:** SEJ25-021, 027, 030.

### SEJ-BANDING: Membandingkan sumber atau keadaan (level 2)

- **Asks:** how two sources differ in character, or how life differed before and after an event.
- **Stem patterns:** "Bagaimana perbedaan karakteristik antara dua bahan yang digunakan siswa tersebut?" · "Berdasarkan informasi diatas, bagaimana perbandingan persatuan dan nasionalisme di masyarakat sebelum dan sesudah Sumpah Pemuda?"
- **Key:** both sides of the comparison stated in parallel: a speech text is the direct view of a participant, a later book is a retrospective interpretation (008); before Sumpah Pemuda the struggle was regional, after it a sense of one nation arose (012).
- **Distractors:** a judgment of which source is better instead of how they differ (008, options A–D); "no change" (012, option C); the national feeling placed before the event (012, options D and E); a reversed aim (012, option B, struggling "untuk kolonialisme").
- **Examples:** SEJ25-008, 012.

### SEJ-SUMBER: Menafsirkan sumber (level 3)

- **Asks:** what a relief, table, quotation or historian's statement shows, or which interpretations of it are right.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda` or `benar-salah`.
- **Stimulus:** the source itself, with a source line; the figure description must name every detail the key uses.
- **Stem patterns:** "Apa penggambaran yang tepat dari Relief Karmawibhangga Panel nomor 65 tentang kehidupan sosial masyarakat?" · "Berdasarkan informasi di atas, manakah penafsiran yang paling tepat mengenai Sumpah Palapa bagi Majapahit?" · "Berdasarkan data tersebut, manakah dampak negatif akibat kebijakan pemerintah pada masa Demokrasi Parlementer?" · "Berdasarkan kutipan tersebut, apa kelemahan utama dari kebijakan Politik Mercusuar ...?"
- **Key:** a claim the source supports directly: the quote's "keunggulan Indonesia di mata dunia" and "krisis ekonomi semakin parah" give 029 option C.
- **Distractors:** something the source does not show (010, options C and D); the reverse of the source (025, statement 2, a positive result offered as a negative one; 029, options D and E, successes); a narrowed reading (014, statement 2, "wilayah daratan" only).
- **Watch:** the image must be legible: the animals in SEJ25-010's relief were identified from a small picture. Check every attribution (029).
- **Examples:** SEJ25-010, 014, 025, 029.

### SEJ-RELEVAN: Relevansi gagasan tokoh masa kini (level 3)

- **Asks:** which present-day actions reflect a national figure's ideas.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda-kompleks` (choose 2–3 of 5).
- **Stimulus:** the figure and the ideas, named explicitly (Ki Hajar Dewantara: pendidikan, pers, diplomasi, Taman Siswa; Mohammad Hatta: diplomasi, pendidikan, koperasi, ekonomi kerakyatan).
- **Stem patterns:** "Manakah pernyataan berikut yang mencerminkan ide Ki Hajar Dewantara dan masih relevan untuk kehidupan masa kini?" · "Jika Anda adalah seorang pemuda yang ingin menghidupkan semangat Mohammad Hatta di masa kini, manakah tindakan yang tepat ...?"
- **Key:** each keyed action maps to one idea named in the stimulus (020: koperasi digital → koperasi; berdiplomasi → diplomasi; ekonomi kerakyatan).
- **Distractors:** the opposite of the idea (020, options C and E, foreign investors or big corporations dominating; 026, options A and B, programmes for big business and deregulation); a value the figure did not hold (004, option B "segala cara", option C keeping every tradition).
- **Examples:** SEJ25-004, 020, 026.

## 4. Known weak spots in the 2025 paper

Avoid repeating these in generated items:

- Indicator boxes that do not match the item: SEJ25-013 (indicator on comparing two sources, item on the Mataram–Surabaya conflict), 014 (elemen "Perlawanan terhadap Bangsa Eropa" on a Majapahit item), 025 (indicator on Demokrasi Terpimpin, table on Demokrasi Liberal). Many boxes are also cut off.
- Items printed twice (SEJ25-007, 009).
- Doubtful facts and attributions (SEJ25-027 date, 029 source).
- Keys that a teacher must check: 5 of 30 have `keyakinan_kunci` = `rendah` (SEJ25-002, 007, 018, 021, 024).
