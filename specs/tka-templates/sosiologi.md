# TKA template: Sosiologi

Describes the TKA 2025 Sosiologi paper (pilihan, 30 items, numbers 1–30, none missing) as 6 item types. Codes, levels and examples are in `data/tka-types.json`.

## 1. The paper at a glance

- **Case first.** Apart from the four items whose stimulus is mainly a figure or table (SOS25-006, 011, 013, 018), every item opens with a social case; case and stem together run 50–132 words. Cases are usually set in a fictional place (Desa Tanjung Harapan, Kelurahan Maju Jaya, Kota Bahari, Kota Madani, Kampung Cempaka, Desa Wirausaha). A few use real material with a source line: Indische Partij and Sumpah Pemuda (SOS25-003, nationalgeographic.grid.id), air pollution (029, BBC Indonesia).
- **Figures and tables.** Five items have a figure: two infographics (006 urbanisasi, 018 jenis kelompok sosial), a bar chart (011), and two illustrations (016 a protest against a mine, 027 a phone marked "HOAX"). One has a table of informant quotes by theme (013). One infographic from KemenPPPA (SOS25-T01) is printed before items 7 and 8, but neither item needs it.
- **Formats.** 27 `pilihan-ganda`, 2 `pilihan-ganda-kompleks` (007, 010), 1 `benar-salah` (005, columns Benar / Salah).
- **Content.** Each item names an *elemen* (Sosiologi sebagai Ilmu; Hubungan dan Gejala Sosial; Penelitian Sosial; Kelompok Sosial, Kesetaraan, dan Konflik Sosial; Perubahan Sosial dan Globalisasi). Item 12 has no indicator box; the source prints only "Soal No.12 (Elemen Soal No.5)".
- **Similar options.** Many items offer five plausible sociological statements that differ in emphasis. 5 of 30 keys have `keyakinan_kunci` = `rendah` (SOS25-010, 019, 023, 025, 030).

## 2. Rules for every Sosiologi item

- **Distractors must be wrong by concept, not by degree.** The strong items name a different concept in each option: SOS25-004 (asimilasi, kontravensi, koersi, persaingan, akomodasi), 014 (konflik antarkelas, antarkelompok kepentingan, politik, budaya, antarpribadi), 021 (five kinds of kesetaraan). The weak ones offer five positive statements of the same kind: 025 (B, C and E all describe openness to global contacts).
- **Only one cause, effect or step may be in the case.** SOS25-016 asks for the "penyebab utama", but the case gives both "tidak dilibatkan dalam proses perizinan" (option D) and the clash over hutan adat (key E). 019 options A and D both restate the case's unequal participation.
- **The stem must name the groups and events as the case does.** SOS25-019 asks about tension "antara kelompok pendatang dan etnis minoritas", while the case describes tension between those two groups and the penduduk asli.
- **Research cases must state every detail the options use.** SOS25-009 does not say how the 150 respondents were chosen, yet the options turn on sampling techniques, and the key's "simple random sampling" sits badly with the case's selection criteria (usia 13–18, sering memakai media sosial).
- **Use a shared stimulus only if the items need it.** The KemenPPPA infographic before SOS25-007 and 008 plays no part in either.
- **Real data is cited and correct, with units.** SOS25-029 prints the WHO standard as "5 μg/m" (should be μg/m³), and its figures from BBC are unchecked. Invented data belongs in clearly fictional places.
- **Indonesian textbook terms.** Use *interaksi sosial*, *kontak sosial*, *akomodasi*, *asimilasi*, *kelompok primer/sekunder*, *gejala sosial*, *kesetaraan kesempatan*, *penelitian kualitatif/kuantitatif*, as the Kurikulum Merdeka books do.

## 3. Item types

### SOS-DATA: Membaca data hasil penelitian (level 1)

- **Asks:** what a chart or table of research results shows.
- **Stimulus:** a chart with every value given in the figure description (SOS25-011: Daur Ulang Sampah 50, Penanaman Pohon 80, Bersih Sungai 120).
- **Stem pattern:** "Berdasarkan grafik hasil penelitian di atas, penjelasan manakah yang paling tepat mengenai pola partisipasi komunitas dalam kegiatan lingkungan?"
- **Key:** a statement exactly true of the data (the highest category).
- **Distractors:** the lowest category called the highest (option D); "uniform" or "no difference" when the values differ (options A and E); a general claim the data does not support (option C).
- **Example:** SOS25-011.

### SOS-KONSEP: Konsep sosiologi dalam kasus (level 2)

- **Asks:** which concept a case shows (bentuk hubungan sosial, bentuk konflik, jenis kelompok, prinsip kesetaraan, syarat interaksi), or why the case counts as an instance of a concept (gejala sosial, multikulturalisme, kelompok sosial).
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda`, or `benar-salah` with 3 statements (005).
- **Stem patterns:** "Berdasarkan ilustrasi tersebut, bentuk hubungan sosial apakah yang akhirnya terwujud di antara kedua kelompok masyarakat di atas?" · "Apa bentuk konflik sosial yang terjadi berdasarkan ilustrasi tersebut?" · "Mengapa sumpah pemuda pada kasus tersebut bisa dikategorikan sebagai simbol multikulturalisme?" · "Apa yang memungkinkan interaksi sosial tetap terjadi pada masa pandemi ...?" · "Mengapa peristiwa yang terjadi dalam ilustrasi tersebut dianggap sebagai gejala sosial?"
- **Key:** the concept and the feature of the case that proves it, in one option ("Akomodasi, karena terdapat upaya meredakan ketegangan dengan kesepakatan bersama dan saling membantu").
- **Distractors:** a neighbouring concept (asimilasi for akomodasi in 004, option A, refuted by "tetap mempertahankan budaya masing-masing"); an earlier stage of the same case (004, option B, kontravensi); a means or condition offered as the concept (002, options A, D, E: technology, internet, handphone instead of komunikasi); the infographic's labels swapped between examples (018, options A–D); a cause the case does not state (001, options A, B, D); the concept's opposite (003, options A and B, loss of identity).
- **Watch:** when the stem says "akhirnya", the case must state the final state plainly, as SOS25-004 does.
- **Examples:** SOS25-001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 012, 014, 018, 021.

### SOS-METODE: Penelitian sosial (level 2)

- **Asks:** the aim, method, stage, steps or benefit of a described piece of research.
- **Formats:** `pilihan-ganda` or `pilihan-ganda-kompleks`.
- **Stimulus:** a research description (aim, subjects, data collection) or a table of results.
- **Stem patterns:** "Manakah pernyataan yang menunjukkan tujuan penelitian kualitatif studi kasus dari permasalahan sosial tersebut?" · "Manakah urutan langkah-langkah penelitian yang termasuk tahapan pelaksanaan penelitian sosial?" · "Manakah pernyataan yang menunjukkan metode penelitian yang sesuai dengan teknik pengumpulan data yang dilakukan?" · "Bagaimana langkah-langkah penelitian yang tepat agar peneliti bisa menghasilkan sajian data tersebut?"
- **Key:** follows the textbook distinctions: qualitative aims identify and understand, quantitative aims measure korelasi or pengaruh (007); pelaksanaan is collecting and analysing data (008); themed informant quotes come from an interview guide and categorisation (013, option A).
- **Distractors:** the other paradigm's aim or steps (007, options C and E; 013, options B, C, E); a step from persiapan or pelaporan (008); a theoretical benefit when a practical one is asked (010, options A and C); an outcome of policy rather than of research (010, option E).
- **Watch:** write steps as numbered statements and let options combine numbers; SOS25-008 joins two full sentences with "dan" in every option. State the sampling if an option names it (009).
- **Examples:** SOS25-007, 008, 009, 010, 013.

### SOS-SEBAB: Penyebab atau pemicu masalah sosial (level 2)

- **Asks:** the main cause or first trigger of a conflict or problem, or why one condition leads to another.
- **Stem patterns:** "Berdasarkan ilustrasi tersebut, apa penyebab utama terjadinya konflik sosial di Desa Tanjung Harapan?" · "Mengapa ketimpangan sosial dapat meningkatkan angka kemiskinan pada masyarakat perkotaan pada kasus tersebut?" · "Mengapa globalisasi pada kasus tersebut dapat menimbulkan kesenjangan sosial?" · "Mengapa masalah sosial dalam ilustrasi tersebut merupakan dampak dari globalisasi?"
- **Key:** the mechanism the case gives (022, option D: unequal access to basic services and policy that does not favour residents; 027, option D: fast, mass spread of information across borders).
- **Distractors:** blaming the affected group (016, option C; 022, options B, C, E; 023, option E); a cause outside the case (016, options A and B; 023, option A); a claim about globalisation that does not explain the spread of the hoax (027, options A, B, C, E).
- **Watch:** this type has the most doubtful keys (SOS25-016, 019, 023). Build the case so that one cause comes first and the others follow from it.
- **Examples:** SOS25-016, 019, 022, 023, 027.

### SOS-DAMPAK: Dampak gejala atau perubahan sosial (level 2)

- **Asks:** the effect of a social phenomenon, inequality or social change, sometimes the long-term effect.
- **Stem patterns:** "Jika hal tersebut terus terjadi, apa dampak negatif gejala sosial yang relevan berdasarkan kasus pada infografis?" · "Apakah dampak sosial yang mungkin terjadi akibat adanya ketidakpuasan masyarakat miskin terhadap layanan publik?" · "Apa bentuk dampak globalisasi yang paling menonjol terhadap kehidupan ekonomi masyarakat?" · "Apa dampak jangka panjang dari pola interaksi sosial yang mulai berubah di Kampung Cempaka?"
- **Key:** the effect at the end of the chain the stem names. In SOS25-020 the stem asks about the effect of dissatisfaction (protest, option A), not of inequality; in 006 urbanisation reduces the village's role as food supplier, so prices rise (option D).
- **Distractors:** the effect of the earlier link in the chain (020, options B and C); the effect of a different process (006, options B and C, industrialisation in the village); the reverse of the case (006, option A; 024, options A, B, D, E); a symptom already present offered as a long-term effect (026, options B and D); a claim the case contradicts (026, option A, while the area became cleaner).
- **Watch:** SOS25-025 has five positive, similar consequences. Give each distractor a different, checkable error.
- **Examples:** SOS25-006, 020, 024, 025, 026.

### SOS-SOLUSI: Strategi, sikap, atau rekomendasi (level 3)

- **Asks:** the best strategy, attitude or recommendation for a stated actor facing a social problem.
- **Stem patterns:** "Bagaimana strategi yang tepat untuk mencapai kedamaian yang berkelanjutan antara komunitas adat dan perusahaan perkebunan?" · "Sikap manakah yang paling sesuai untuk membangun kesadaran siswa agar tetap menghargai budaya daerah tanpa menolak budaya luar?" · "Apa rekomendasi yang paling tepat diberikan kepada institusi pendidikan tinggi agar bisa berkontribusi dalam mengatasi masalah tersebut?" · "Apa rekomendasi paling strategis yang bisa dilakukan pemuda Desa Trunyan untuk menjaga nilai budaya di tengah arus globalisasi?"
- **Key:** fits the stage of the problem, the actor and the kind of answer asked: after failed mediation, arbitrase (015, option C); for a university facing missing data, a research centre (029, option C); an attitude when the stem asks for "sikap" (028, option C).
- **Distractors:** a weaker step than one already tried (015, options A, B, E); coercion or ignoring the objection (017, options A, D, E); a step that deepens the stated threat (030, options B, D, E, more commercialisation); an action outside the actor's role or off the problem (029, options A, D, E); an activity when an attitude is asked (028, options A, B, D).
- **Watch:** in SOS25-017 the case already narrates the solution, so the item is a reading task. In 030 options A and C both protect culture; the case must say which threat to address.
- **Examples:** SOS25-015, 017, 028, 029, 030.

## 4. Known weak spots in the 2025 paper

Avoid repeating these in generated items:

- An indicator box that does not match the item (SOS25-005: "proses terbentuknya kelompok sosial" on a gejala sosial item) or is missing (012).
- A shared infographic that no item uses (SOS25-T01 before 007 and 008).
- Typos in options: "Tedapat" (003), "samplel cluster samping" (009), "pedang tradisional" (014), "yeng" (020), "nagara-negara" (029).
